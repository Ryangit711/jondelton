The Elton Lake House

Sudden Valley · Lake Whatcom · Bellingham, Washington

Our Family Gathering Place

Enjoy the waterfront of beautiful Lake Whatcom — waterski, swim, kayak, canoe, or just read a book. Located at #6 Inlet Circle, Bellingham, in Sudden Valley on Lake Whatcom.

A 4,200 sq. ft. home with 5 bedrooms and 4½ bathrooms, including the self-contained suite on the lowest level. It has been a great family reunion location for our family of 6 grown children, their spouses, and 15 grandchildren.

As you can imagine, it is pretty tight when we are all there at the same time — but it’s great having everyone under one roof.
