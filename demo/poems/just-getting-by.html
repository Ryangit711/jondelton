Just Getting By

D.H. Elton

I met him one morning, As I went to town. He was young, he was strong, But somewhat cast down. I greeted him thus: "How are you, my boy?" He answered me languidly: "Just getting by". Just getting by! That's not enough. Just getting by! Is pretty poor stuff. The driftwood, the derelicts, The shiftless, the shy, Are the fatal results of Just getting by. If you're "just getting by", You're not fit for the race, When the boys "toe the mark", You won't find a place. With the sprinters and winners, You never can match. When the gun cracks the signal -- You're left at the scratch! The leader, the lifters, The builders, were they Who were doing, pursuing Their work, night and day. They scorned the excuses Of wherefore and why, And tabooed the apology; "Just getting by". The soul, and the source Of Success, surely springs From ambition, initiative, Work -- DOING THINGS! It is never attained by Those who won't try, But are simply content with "Just getting by".
