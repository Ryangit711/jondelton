My Mother

D.H. Elton

My Mother Guardian of Baby-hood, Boy-hood, and Youth. Champion of Virtue, And Honour and Truth. Friend of my Manhood — Eternal Friend, aye Her spirit and influence, Bless me, each day — MY MOTHER. In the “Mansions of Glory,” A Friend is she. With the “Sanctified Hosts” She is pleading for me. She links me with Heaven, With God, and His will, She was always my Friend And my Friend she is still — MY MOTHER. Death does not finish Her mission of Love, She zealously labours In regions above — There — through all time And Eternity, Her Love shall endure, And forever she’ll be — MY MOTHER. •D.H. Elton• — 1935 This is a photo of Isabella Amelia Elton with her Son Hyrum, Grandpa's brother, and his two daughters.
