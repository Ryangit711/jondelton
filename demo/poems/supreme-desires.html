Supreme Desires

D.H. Elton

I'd rather have friends than money, I'd rather my life were told In deeds of kindness and service, I'd rather have conscience than gold, I'd rather be counted cheerful, I'd rather be heard in song, I'd rather be known as a "jolly old crone" Who tries to help others along. I'd rather the love of dear children, I'd rather their tender caress- Knowing their innocent sweetness, Seeketh and loveth the best. For the love, and affection of children, And song, and conscience, and friend And the lilt of a laugh, are the rod and the staff, Giving comfort, and strength to the end.
