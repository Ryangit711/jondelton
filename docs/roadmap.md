## After Launch — Maintenance Rhythm

- **Weekly:** automatic backups run in the background.
- **Monthly:** update WordPress core + plugins (one click).
- **Monthly:** publish "Gems from Jon" (now free to send).
- **Quarterly:** quick performance check (PageSpeed, GTmetrix).

## What We Will NOT Do

- We will **not** rebuild the site from scratch and abandon WordPress unless Jon asks.
- We will **not** take on paid hosting changes or contracts without Jon's approval.
- We will **not** rush the launch — Jon's words deserve a careful hand.
