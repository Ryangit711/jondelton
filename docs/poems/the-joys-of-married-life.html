The Joys of Married Life

D.H. Elton

The joys of married life; The bliss of matrimony; The comfort of a wife; Without her - Oh! how lonely! A baby sweet to kiss, A mother dear to love, Brings happiness and bliss And peace from heav'n above. The days may gloomy be, Life's work with care abound, But, once at home we're free, With them sweet rest is found. Cares flee as morning dew Before the summer's sun, Hope springs within anew, With wife and babe at home!
