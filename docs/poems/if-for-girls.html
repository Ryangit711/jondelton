“If” For Girls

D.H. Elton

If you can keep your beau, when girls around you Are losing theirs, and envying lucky you, If you can neatly dress, and yet be modest, With just a bit of paint and powder, too. If you can "bob" your hair, and not look foolish If you can walk erect in modern shoes, You'll be a right smart girl, of real good promise-- The sort of girl a chap would wish to choose. If you can keep your lips free from tobacco-- Your finger-tips from telltale nicotine If you can keep your nails, not over polished, But just enough to keep them trim and clean, If you are worldly-wise, and yet not worldly, and keep your mental furniture in shape, Respect your ego--without egoism, Be what you are, and never fake, or ape. If you can mix, and yet remain untarnished, And not be a wet blanket, with you group. If you can hold your own in jolly frolic, And yet to no mean lower-level stoop, If you can keep a sense of goodly humour, Enjoy a joke, and join in wholesome fun, You'll keep your beau--if ye be worth the keeping, And joy be yours, through all the years to come.
