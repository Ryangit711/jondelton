Have You Tried?

D.H. Elton

In the struggles and strife of everyday life, When 'tis apparently useless to try, For you are minus all hope you'll get over the slope, And you just want to crawl off and die; Then's the time to say 'Scat,' and get up to the bat And "wallop the pill" for a run. You won't make a hit, so long as you sit On the sidelines and squawk: "Can't be done!" Here's a lesson for you, and Brother, it's true. Just hear the Good Shepherd declare-- It's the best test at length, revealing the strength Of your faith and devotion and prayer. Poor Peter had fished, all night he had wished Some fish to his empty nets come, But the night passed away and the dawn of the day Found him fishless, and dishless, and glum. Next day, came the call, from the Good Lord of all, And His pulpit he made in the boat, So the fold on the shore heard the voice they adored, As He taught them the Gospel of Hope. When finished, said He: Peter, put out to sea, And let down your nets at My word." But Peter replied: "All night we have tried, And nothing have taken, my Lord." "But nevertheless, at Thy Holy behest, We'll cast down our nets once again," And so they obeyed, and were amply repaid, For their nets couldn't quite stand the strain. So remember it's best when discouraged, depressed, To go forward with colors a flying, For the crown never sits, on the fellow that quits, But's reserved for the ones that keep trying.
