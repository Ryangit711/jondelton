Teach Me to Love
								

				

				

				

									
Four little words, a simple phrase,
That falls like dew on sleeping flower,
Or, like the sun’s effulgent rays,
Bestowing Service every hour.
For, in these simple words, I find
The Light and Wisdom from above—
The essence of all good, combined
In these four words: “Teach me to love.”

“Teach me to love” with heart sincere,
Devoid of all dissimulation.
A love that gladly volunteers
To Serve, without remuneration.
For, in such Love, as I conclude,
We lift our thoughts to Heaven above
In gentle, joyful rectitude
Expressed in Faith: “Teach me to love.”

“Teach me to love,” both here and now,
Humanity as one great whole.
Who waits upon the “When” and “How”
Will lose the race and miss the goal.
Loving and Serving, day by day,
With humble grace, in semblance of
Our Hope—the Light, the Truth, the Way,
Still guided by: “Teach me to love.”

•D.H. Elton•
