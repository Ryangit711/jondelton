Enjoy the waterfront of beautiful lake Whatcom. Waterski, swim, kayak, canoe or just read a book...
				

				

					

		

				

			

						

				

									
Located at #6 Inlet Circle, Bellingham, in Sudden Valley on Lake Whatcom.  A 4200 sq. foot home with 5 bedrooms and 4 and 1/2 bathrooms. (Including the self contained suite on the lowest level.) It has been a great family reunion location for our family of 6 grown children, their spouses and 15 grand children.  (As you can imagine, it is pretty tight when we are all there at the same time but it's great having everyone under one roof.)
								

				

					

		

					

		

				

						

					

			

						

				

							

			

		

						

				

					

		

				

			

						

				

					
The Main Level
				

				

				

				

									

The house is on three levels with the bottom level a self contained apartment.  The upper 2 floors have 4 bedrooms, 3 and a half baths and can be rented as one unit.

The entry level, top floor, or main level, has a large kitchen, dining and living room area, a master bedroom with king sized bed and ensuite bathroom, with a large deck looking out on the lake and Sofield Park.  There is also a 2 piece powder room on that floor and access to the spacious double garage.  

								

				

					

		

					

		

				

						

					

			

						

				

					
The Second Floor
				

				

				

				

									

The second or middle floor has 3 bedrooms each with queen sized beds, 7 bunk beds and various couches, a hide a bed, and two full bathrooms. It also has a family room area with a large screen TV, children’s play area and a large deck with fun hammock chairs and great views of the lake and park. We have lots of musical instruments books and puzzles for the children to play with.  This is the ‘fun’ area of the house!

								

				

					

		

				

			

						

				

							

			

		

						

				

					

		

					

		

				

						

					

			

						

				

							

			

		

						

				

					

		

				

			

						

				

					
The Basement Suite
				

				

				

				

									

The self contained apartment, on the bottom level has ‘walk-out’ access to the lake a bedroom with a queen sized bed, a fully equipped kitchen and a large common area with a hide-a-bed, large sectional and great flat screen TV.  It can be separate from the rest of the house and can nicely accommodate a family of 4-6. 

								

				

					

		

					

		

				

						

					

			

						

				

					
Lots of Fun for the whole Family!
				

				

				

				

							

			

		

						

				

				

				

									

This house is ideal for family reunions.  The patriarch and matriarch take the comfortable master suite on the top floor with the children and grand children on the floor, or floors below.  There is plenty of room in the house for inside activities and it is well stocked with games, videos, puzzles and musical instruments.  (There’s a great area for family talent shows!)  The weather is not always perfect and it is nice to have interesting inside areas.

Outside is Whatcom lake.  Beautiful!! Our little bay has a beach with low bank access to the beach, a swim platform, and party island.  We have lots of water toys including 2 canoes, a kayak, a paddle board and lots of ‘blow-up’ children toys and life jackets for every shape and size.  The house is on a small park which has a picnic table, fire pit, volleyball poles, and an open area for playing catch, soccer, Bocce ball, or Frisbee Golf.  This whole area is a wild life preserve so there are lots of deer and wild life.  Everything you need for a fabulous family get together!

The address is #6 Inlet Circle, Bellingham  (If you want to Google it) and it is in Sudden Valley Gate 1.  At gate 2, just up Lakeway Drive, there is a gas station, general store, and Italian Restaurant, Tino’s.  (Great Pizza.)  Gate 2 is also the the access to Sudden Valley’s beautiful 18 hole golf course, the YMCA, Mexican Restaurant, Library and other fields and recreational areas.

Bellingham is a nice medium sized town with a large new Costco, 2 Fred Meyers, good movie theatres, restaurants, and a wonderful Farmers Market, Saturday mornings, during the summer.  There is also Old Fairhaven, a lovely old part of town with quaint stores, fun restaurants and one of the best book stores in the world, Village Books.

There are great walking and hiking trails in the area and a lovely park about half way between Sudden Valley and downtown Bellingham called Whatcom Falls Park, where there are trails, Salmon pools, water falls, a wonderful old stone bridge, and beautiful pools with cliff jumping fun for the less faint hearted.  (Just go to the left after crossing the old stone bridge.)  All in all a lot of fun stuff to do both at the lake and in the area.
