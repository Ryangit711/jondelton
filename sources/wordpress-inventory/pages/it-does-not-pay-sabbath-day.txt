It Does Not Pay-

Sabbath Day
								

				

				

				

									
It does not pay, to break the Sabbath Day.
The work you feel In Duty bound to do
Can all be done within the time
God has appointed you.

Six days for Labour-
Seventh for God, Worship, and Rest,
Do this:  Obey the Fourth Command-
Trust and be blessed.

•D.H. Elton•
