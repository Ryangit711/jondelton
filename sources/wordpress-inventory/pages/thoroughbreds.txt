Thoroughbreds
								

				

				

				

									
Way down in ole Kentucky where the corn and ‘taters grow,
In that grand old blue grass region where creeks and rivers flow.
They’ve got some racin’ hosses, jockies find ‘em hard to stop,
For they want to keep on runnin’ with their heads up till they drop.

They say that these are thoroughbreds, they always finish strong,
Endurance is the quality that pushes them along.
They always run for all they are worth - free rein and lots of bit-
The way to tell a thoroughbred-you’ll never see him quit.

Why can’t we  just be thoroughbreds and run for all we’re worth,
In spite of all discouragements, and other things that hurt.
Take a lesson from the hosses-never fluke or flunk, or flop,
But lets just keep on a runnin’ With our heads up ‘til we drop!

•D.H. Elton•
