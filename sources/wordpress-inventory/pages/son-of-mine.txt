Son of Mine
								

				

				

				

									
Written to David Hauser Elton grandpa’s oldest son.  
The last line of the poem is on our family ring designed by Chester and I.
								

				

				

				

									
Do the Right son of Mine, all the time.
In the light of the right always shine.
This test, do your best every day.
Never Fear, God is near, Ever Pray.

Head Erect, self-respect, Conscience free,
Garnish Truth and forsooth Chastity
In your walk, in your talk, Just be straight.
Always work, never shirk, Keep the faith.

•D.H. Elton•
