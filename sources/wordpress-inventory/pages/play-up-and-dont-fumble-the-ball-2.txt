Play up and Don't

Fumble the Ball
								

				

				

				

									

(Written for his son Dalton, when he was playing high school football.)

								

				

				

						

					

			

						

				

															
															

				

					

		

				

			

						

				

									
Life is a game, and life in the main,
Must be played by the rules of the game.
One may cheat for a while, and get by with a smile,
And partake of its glamour and fame.

But, I’m thinking my son, that ‘ere life is done,
You will find it is best after all,
To hit the line square, be honest and fair:-
Play up, and don’t fumble the ball!

There’s a time to finesse, as we freely confess-
If you never lose sight of the goal.
If the master you’d be of your own destiny,
Be sure you are still in control.

If, to weakness you play, it’s a dead “give away,”
You’re fearful and trying to stall-
So with pluck and with grit, hit the line, never quit:
Play up and don’t fumble the ball!

Whatever you do, you’ll find ‘ere you’re through-
The right thing to do is the best.
The right can’t be beat - there’s no need for retreat,
If the right is your every day quest.

Let me once again say, and hope, every day,
These words you will often recall:
With vigour and vim, determined to win:
Play up and don’t fumble the ball!

                                 •D. H. Elton•
