Life, The True Poem
								

				

				

				

									
Let my life be the poem I’d write,
Let it plead for me day after day.
Never mind what my pen may indite,
Never mind what my feeble lips say.

What I do is a heart-felt refrain.
What I am is the standard and test.
The rhythm of action is plain,
The poem I live is the best.

Let me BE let me DO let me LIVE,
The poem my LIFE may inspire.
Let me Strive, let me Serve, let me GIVE,
The Highest my Soul can Desire.
•David H. Elton•
