Three Things
								

				

				

				

									
(Written for my youngest son, John Dalton Elton, on the
occasion of his 14th Birthday, February 27th, 1934, Library, 
at home). Born February 27th, 1920.
								

				

				

				

							

			

		

						

				

				

				

									
Three things,” my son,
To help you on.
They all come from within.
The “first” I’ll state,
A virtue great, is called:
“SELF-DISCIPLINE.”

For number “Two,”
I transmit you,
A “Grace” none should neglect —
Just like it’s mate —
A grand estate, we’ll call it:
“SELF-RESPECT.”

And number “Three,”
You’ll plainly see,
Cast in the self same mold —
A moral will
To conquer ill, is rightly:
“SELF-CONTROL.”

Keep these in mind,
And you will find
True happiness and joy.
They’ll premiums pay
In every way,
Big dividends, my boy.
