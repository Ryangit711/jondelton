The man who never in his life,
Has washed the dishes with his wife,”
Or mopped the kitchen, waxed the floor,
Or shook the rug at the back door,

Or beat the carpet with a broom,
Or “tidied up” the sitting room,
Or carried out the “Garbage Can,”
Or cleaned the “Frig” and scrubbed the pan,

Or made the bed, or swept the stairs,
Or dusted off the dining chairs,
“Or polished up the silver plate,
He still is largely celibate.”

These are a very, very few
Of numerous things that women do —
But in these days with maid, non est —
We might dig in and do our best.

•D.H. Elton• — October 12, 1943
