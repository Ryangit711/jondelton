U and I
								

				

				

				

									

Who keeps the world a going, 
Does the plowing and the sowing, 
And the reaping and the mowing?
U and I.

Who makes the corporation, 
And capital creation,
In every generation?
U and I.

Who saves the situation,
Of every bloomin’ nation,
And makes the grand equation?
U and I.

Who has to pay the bills,
Make the grade and climb the hills 
Buck the storms and take the spills?
U and I.

We’re the doers and the dreamers,
We’re the lifters and the leaners,
We’re the statesmen and the schemers —
U and I.

So, it really doesn’t matter,
‘Bout this governmental chatter; 
We’re the folk who fill the platter —
U and I.

And the world? We can’t escape it, 
We’re the folk who mar, or make it, 
We’re the ones who give, and take it —
U and I.

Thus, a little more of me, 
And a little more of you,
A little more of we,
And a little more of do, 
For, after all my friend — 
There’s only just we two —
U and I.
