My Creed
								

				

				

				

									
Read a little, Write a little,
Speak a little - then
Sing a little, Serve a little,
Help your fellow-men.

Dream a little, Think a little,
Find some Work to do.
Save a little, Spend a little,
Give a little too.

Hope a little, Trust a little,
Have a heap o’ Faith,
Play a little, Pray a little,
Walk a path that’s straight.

Eat a little, Sleep a little,
Keep the body fit.
Laugh a little, chaff a little,
Love a little bit.
•David H. Elton•
