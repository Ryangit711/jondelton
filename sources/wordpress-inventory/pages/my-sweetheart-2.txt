My Sweetheart
								

				

				

				

									
I see you, as in former years,
‘Mid laughter, sadness, joys and tears,
Through shade and sunshine, faith and fears,
The star of hope — Time but endears —
MY SWEETHEART.

And babies, as the years roll on,
With merry chatter, cheery song,
Have helped us on the way along —
Recalling days when we were young —
MY SWEETHEART.

I sometimes wonder how we’d fare,
If we were just a childless pair —
No “boys” or “girls” our lives to share,
No children, dear, to love and care —
MY SWEETHEART.

It seems to me, we’d miss a heap,
Of real joy, and gladness sweet,
Which only “boys” and “girls” entreat
To help o’ercome the cares we meet —
MY SWEETHEART.

You’re still as sweet, so runs my plea,
As that “blest day” in Tennessee,
When just “Seventeen” you did agree,
To have this Photo made for me —
MY SWEETHEART.

I’ve cherished it through all the years,
Faith’s precious gift — dispelling fears,
Symbol of love — “Life’s glad arrears,”
Hope’s blessed rainbow through the tears —
MY SWEETHEART.

•D.H. Elton•
