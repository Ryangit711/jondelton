Home Page
		

		

		
Hey everybody!!  Welcome to my web site.  Not too much here yet, but it is a start.  So far I have put up a lot of Grandpa's poems and a blog of my recent trip to Malaysia and Thailand.   Great trip!  Under the title Jon's Poems and Thoughts there is a collection of all kinds of thoughts and poems I have collected over the years.  There is no real organization to them at this point, but lots of stuff there if you care to wade through it.  At some point I will have to better organize it.  I have also just added My Desiderata.  You might find that interesting.  

The address of the web site is  &lt;&lt;andtheywereright.com&gt;&gt; an address I have owned for a number of years.  It came to me from a fellow by the name of Neale Donald Walsch.  He is mainly known as the author of a book called Conversations with God.  He sends out a daily email I receive called, "I Believe God Wants You to Know" in which he shares a thought he feels is important.  Often in it, he would quote a bit of wisdom from someone else and would add, "and he was right" then give his own take on it.  I thought, "And they were right" was a great line and as I have always enjoyed great thoughts I thought I might come up with a book with that title, filled with great thoughts that I might talk about and share with others.  We shall see if it ever comes to anything, but in the mean time I own the URL and decided to use it for my web site.  It kind of helps justify having owned and paid for it for it all these years&#8230; and it is a bit catchy.

I will add more to the web site as time goes on.  I hope.  In the mean time, if you have any other of grand dad's poems that you think should be included please send them to me.  Or if you have any favourite thoughts you would like to share, please do that also.   Who knows, we might get that book done yet.  In the mean time I would like to thank my son Byron and Victor Tiongco for their help setting up the site and getting me going on it.  I could not do it without them.             -  jon  -
