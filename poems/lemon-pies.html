Lemon Pies!

Collected by D.H. Elton

Lemon Pies! Tonight I’m going to be a Po-et And rhyme my words before you know it And if perchance my looks don’t show it! Just hold your breath and watch me go-it. And as I pen my thoughts tonight Upon this sheet of paper white, My mind reverts to days gone by, Of Custards sweet and Lemon Pie! I’ll tell you Dora, ’tis the truth, If maids would win the handsome youth They’d better your example try, And feed the lads on Lemon Pie! Now you may say, “It’s all a bluff, Men do not care for spicy stuff,” But I’ll tell you, as one who knows From whence the love of mankind flows. The language shall be plain and terse Found in the last lines of this verse, The spring wherein his deep love lies, Is only reached by Lemon Pies! Then! If the spark of love you’d fan Within the bosom of a man! That it may glow and richly burn And to your own sweet heart return! Just stir it up with spices fine, And you will conquer every time, There’ll be no sorrows, sobs or sighs — Just a Paradise of Lemon Pies!
