Son of Mine

D.H. Elton

Do the Right son of Mine, All the time. In the light of the right Always shine. This test, do your best Every day. Never Fear, God is near, Ever Pray. Head Erect, self-respect, Conscience free, Garnish Truth and forsooth Chastity In your walk, in your talk, Just be straight. Always work, never shirk, Keep the faith.
